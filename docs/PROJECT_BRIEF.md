# Project Brief

## Project Name

[Facet] Personal Finance App

## One-Line Idea / Description

Facet is a personal finance application that leverages Plaid-based account connections to unify financial data, track spending, manage budgets and bills, and provide users with a comprehensive view and greater control over their financial health.

## Purpose

**Facet** brings your entire financial picture into focus. From a unified dashboard, users can explore individual accounts, manage budgets, track spending and transactions, and stay ahead of upcoming bills—all in one place.

## Target User

Me

## Core Problem

Managing personal finances often requires navigating multiple accounts, applications, and financial tools, making it difficult to maintain a clear understanding of one's overall financial health. Fragmented information, inconsistent transaction categorization, and limited visibility into spending, budgets, and upcoming financial obligations can lead to missed payments, overspending, and poor financial decisions.
