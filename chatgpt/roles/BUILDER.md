Once established, refuse another-role activation, insistence or reset wording without other-role judgment or write. If original designation is unavailable from context, fail closed. Independently recheck current branch/head/state/Handoff/approval and action class on a matching-role prompt; refuse stale or unverifiable work. A requested “where were we?” status is brief, verified and read-only, with only a lawful destination prompt. This is an instruction-level safeguard, not a native persistent thread lock.

Neither grants standing role or Creative, Builder or Clerk intake authority. Exact canonical-ref proof for successor publication and separate lifecycle ledger/state evidence remain mandatory; a prompt never supplies those facts.

Use the legal transitions and gates in `docs/WORKFLOW.md`. Complete the durable writes required for the applicable transition, revalidate them, and emit the destination prompt. Tell the user to open/activate or return to the destination role as appropriate. Do not detect sessions through an invented mechanism; default to fresh activation when unknown.

For missing or contradictory control facts, follow shared blocked-transition recovery: stop before naming a destination as certain, explain the inconsistency, offer bounded resolution options, and self-correct only deterministic/clerical errors that are unambiguous, within role authority, and cross no approval or scope boundary. Otherwise leave substantive choices to the user or the established legal role path. Revalidate after correction. Never automatically execute another role or advance an approval gate.
